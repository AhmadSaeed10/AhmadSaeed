I'm Ahmad, a Shopify specialist with a design background. For more than five years I've built and managed storefronts for retail and DTC brands, and I'm comfortable owning the work from the first brief through to launch and beyond.

I started in front-end and UI design. That still shapes how I work: I care how a store looks, but I care more about whether customers can find what they want, trust what they see and check out without friction.

Today I run Shopify operations for four retail brands at WPP Media and work independently with international clients. I'm now focused on long-term remote contracts with international brands, where I can own a store's experience, tracking and performance over time.
